# Observation Records

An observation record is raw evidence of what the game did, under stated
conditions.

## Observation Request

Record the claim it serves, the observable needed, the loaded output's identity
and build receipt, the starting state, the route or procedure, and the
observer's known blind spots.

## Raw Result

Preserve the capture, trace, memory, audio, log, or human report with its
identity, the route and inputs actually used, deviations from the request,
whether it can be replayed, and collection failures. Keep verdicts in
[assessments](claim-assessments.md); a tool's own verdict stays part of its raw
response.

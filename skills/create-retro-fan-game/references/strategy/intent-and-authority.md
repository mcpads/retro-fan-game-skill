# Intent And Authority

Help the person choose an experience and judge the result without needing engine
knowledge, and keep their decisions throughout the work.

## Make The Target Concrete

From the request and earlier decisions, identify the desired experience, the
intended route, what must stay unchanged, and what would count as enough.
Resolve what the next action needs; investigation can start before the design
is complete.

When intent is unclear, study the source context and prepare a small scene,
dialogue variant, visual comparison, or playable moment. Explain its effect on
character, tone, interaction, or lost content, and label samples as mockups or
observed game output. State engine constraints only as measured.

For example, saving a character may raise whether later chapters acknowledge
their survival. Show a short scene and the affected routes so the person can
choose the meaning; when the request already settles it, proceed.

Ask early about uncertainty that affects meaning or scope, and continue
independent work meanwhile. Technical methods are the agent's to choose.

## Authority Boundary

The request authorizes the investigation and routine implementation it needs:
choose methods, repair defects, and draft within its creative constraints while
keeping the requested experience.

The person resolves choices that change premise, character, meaning, canon,
tone, scope, quality, or acceptable loss. Explain the visible effects and
recommend an option with reasons. A technical limit justifies proposing a
tradeoff; the person chooses it. While a choice is pending, pause only the work
that depends on it.

## Carry Approval Forward

Explicit requests and prior choices are approvals for their stated scope, with
or without a formal record; a choice whose authorization cannot be recovered
stays pending. Direction approval permits drafting and
implementation; the resulting content is reviewed at the agreed scope, such as a
scene. The person may delegate lower-impact creative choices; record and keep to
that scope.

Reopen a decision only when work crosses its scope, evidence changes its
premises, or the person revises it, and explain what changed. Record decisions
in the [Approval Record](../conventions/artifact-states.md#approval-record).

## Help The Person Judge The Result

Present the changed experience in context, and ask the person about fit, voice,
and acceptable loss; do the technical investigation and mechanical checks
yourself. Prepare candidates under [Creative Review](creative-review.md). When
human observation is needed, give a short route and the specific result to
report.

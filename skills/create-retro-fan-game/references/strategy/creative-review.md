# Creative Review

The person judges appearance, sound, voice, and meaning. This loop prepares
candidates they can judge quickly and keeps their decisions binding. Authority
comes from [Intent And Authority](intent-and-authority.md).

## Prepare A Judgeable Candidate

- Show the candidate as the player meets it in its segment: native resolution
  and palette, actual motion order and timing, the in-game screen, or a listening
  excerpt at its transition. Enlarged views supplement the native view.
- Place the original or approved reference beside it, and state what changed,
  what stayed, and what is still unreviewed.
- Label it as a mockup, a converted asset, or observed game output.

## Reference Identity

Record where each style, pose, or sound reference came from: the original release
and revision, another edition, an earlier project output, or a generated
candidate. A capture shows the build it was taken from. When a reference proves
misidentified, withdraw the candidates that depend on it and keep them as
history.

## Self-review

Check measurable constraints, such as size, palette, tiles, budgets, and
continuity with the adopted set, and fix obvious defects before presenting. For
animation, play it back and check positional drift, a shared ground line, one
scale per motion against the adopted idle, and counts of eyes, hands, and limbs.
Self-review, including a separate reviewer agent, reports inspection; approval
comes from the person.

After a rejection of something self-review passed, note which judgment missed,
and compare against the source again before the next candidate rather than
patching the rejected one.

## Keep Decisions Binding

- Record acceptance, rejection with its reason, and review priorities in the
  [Approval Record](../conventions/artifact-states.md#approval-record).
- Move rejected candidates out of current selections and into history.
- Approval comes only from the person's reply; while it is pending, continue
  independent work.
- Carry an approved asset through [Workflow Gates](workflow-gates.md) to show
  the game consumes it.

## Review Order

Follow the person's order. Otherwise review coverage and large differences first,
such as missing states, proportions, and identity across a set, then local
detail.

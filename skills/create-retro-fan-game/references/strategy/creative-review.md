# Creative Review

Appearance, sound, voice, and meaning are judged by the person. This loop
prepares candidates they can judge quickly and keeps their decisions binding.
Authority for each decision comes from
[Intent And Authority](intent-and-authority.md).

## Prepare A Judgeable Candidate

- Present the candidate as the player will meet it: native resolution and
  palette, actual motion order and timing, the in-game screen, or a listening
  excerpt at the intended transition. Enlarged views help inspection; they do
  not replace the native view.
- Place the relevant original or approved reference beside it. State what
  changed, what stayed identical, and what remains unreviewed.
- Label whether it is a mockup, a statically converted asset, or observed game
  output. Do not present a mockup as game output.

## Reference Identity

A style, pose, or sound reference is evidence about a source. Record its origin:
the original release and revision, another edition, an earlier project output,
or a generated candidate. A capture from a modified build shows that build, even
when it resembles the original. When a reference proves misidentified, withdraw
the claims and candidates that depend on it and keep them as history. Record
reference provenance with
[asset provenance](../conventions/artifact-states.md#authored-or-generated-asset-provenance).

## Self-review

Check measurable constraints, such as size, palette, tiles, budgets, and
continuity with the adopted set, and remove obvious defects before presenting.
Self-review records the agent's inspection; it cannot approve appearance,
expression, voice, or musical fit.

When the person rejects a result the agent judged acceptable, record which
judgment was wrong. Compare against the source more closely before the next
candidate instead of continuing local fixes to the rejected one.

## Keep Decisions Binding

- Record acceptance, rejection with its reason, and review priorities under the
  [Approval Record](../conventions/artifact-states.md#approval-record).
- Remove rejected candidates from current selections and review sets while
  keeping them as history.
- Revision count, generation effort, or time without a reply is not approval.
  A pending review leaves the unit unapproved; continue independent work.
- Approval does not claim game consumption; carry the adopted asset through
  [Workflow Gates](workflow-gates.md).

## Review Order

Follow the person's stated order. Otherwise review coverage and large
differences first, such as missing states, proportions, and identity across a
set, before local detail, so detail work is not lost to later structural change.

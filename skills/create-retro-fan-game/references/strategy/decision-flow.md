# Decision Flow

Start from the intended playable, the segment in progress, and its current open
question. Choose the smallest action whose result can change what happens next.
Work may branch or return to investigation.

## Select The Branch

| Open question | Useful next result | Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What does the person want to experience or preserve? | A contextual sample or consequential choice | [Intent And Authority](intent-and-authority.md) |
| What work and evidence are currently selected? | Verified current entry | [Project State](../conventions/project-state.md) |
| What does the next segment need, and which source meanings and host surfaces constrain it? | Defined segment with bounded components | [Creative Overlay](creative-overlay.md) |
| What does the engine path allow? | Measured fact or bounded experiment | [Technical Investigation](technical-investigation.md) |
| Which technical operation does the next decision need? | Work role with explicit inputs and claim | [Work Roles](../conventions/work-roles.md) |
| Do the selected changes form one reproducible image? | Cumulative build and receipt | [Execution Records](../conventions/execution-records.md#product-build-path) |
| Does the game use the change correctly? | Evidence separating expected behavior from failure | [Runtime Observation](runtime-observation.md) |
| Does the person accept how it looks, sounds, or reads? | Judgeable candidate and recorded decision | [Creative Review](creative-review.md) |
| Is the segment or route ready to expand or complete? | Scoped assessment | [Workflow Gates](workflow-gates.md) |
| Can the work be shared? | Package inventory and reconstruction evidence | [Optional Patch Package](patch-package.md) |

## Choose Work That Changes The Decision

Inspect an unknown field before building around it, prove a representative unit
before producing a corpus, and observe a failing route before diagnosing from
intermediate data. Connect the segment with placeholders before polishing while
progression risk is unknown. A proven surface covers its own loader and format;
a different one needs its own proof. Advance independent branches while another
waits for evidence or a decision.

When a result is ambiguous, keep it and choose an observation that separates the
remaining explanations. Keep the person's completion target fixed: investigate
further or present the actual tradeoff.

After a result, update its owning record and the
[current entry](../conventions/project-state.md), adopt what the evidence
supports, invalidate affected dependencies, and pick the next open question
against the intended playable.

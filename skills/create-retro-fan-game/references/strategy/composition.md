# Capability Composition

Assign responsibilities so creative authority, execution, and observation stay
replaceable, even within one agent.

## Responsibilities

| Responsibility | Decides or records | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creative authority | Intended experience and creative tradeoffs | [Intent And Authority](intent-and-authority.md) |
| Execution | Declared transforms and their mechanical checks | [Execution Records](../conventions/execution-records.md) |
| Observation | Raw results, conditions, and limits | [Observation Records](../conventions/observation-records.md) |
| Assessment | Whether the evidence satisfies a claim | [Claim Assessments](../conventions/claim-assessments.md) |

One record may hold separate sections for a transform, its observation, and its
assessment. Raw evidence stays unchanged when its interpretation changes.

## Select Capabilities From The Claim

Define the observations that would separate success, failure, and uncertainty,
then choose tools, analysis, or human observation that can make that separation.
When no available method can, look for another or keep the claim open; the
person's requested experience stays the claim.

## Optional Domain-capability Handoffs

Narrative source conformance, cross-surface asset coherence, world reachability,
and combat behavior each need their own evidence. Activate one for the component
that claims it. The current agent may do this analysis itself or with a
compatible skill. Reference the specialized plan and its assessment through the
[Capability Handoff Record](../conventions/artifact-states.md#capability-handoff-record);
execution, observation, packaging, and final creative authority stay here.

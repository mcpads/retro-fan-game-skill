# The person rejected a result the agent's self-review accepted

- **Search terms:** rejected expression, self-review, reviewer agent, revision count, local pixel fixes, face breakage, animation drift, aesthetic approval
- **Misleading observation:** Several rounds of local fixes, or a separate reviewer agent, judged a candidate acceptable, and the recorded edit effort suggests progress.
- **Other interpretation:** Self-review caught measurable defects but did not predict the person's judgment of expression, and contact sheets hid motion defects such as drift, scale changes, or extra limbs.
- **Distinguishing check:** Compare the original at native size and magnification, play motion back, and change the smallest region that addresses the stated rejection instead of continuing edits on the rejected result.
- **Transfer limit:** The specific visual choices do not transfer. Report self-review only as inspection.

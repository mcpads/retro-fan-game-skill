# The person rejected a result the agent's self-review accepted

- **Search terms:** rejected expression, self-review, revision count, local pixel fixes, face breakage, aesthetic approval
- **Observed scope:** Expression and pose revisions of one pixel-art character for battle poses, reviewed by the person through native and enlarged HTML review pages.
- **Failure context:** Several rounds of local fixes were each self-reviewed as acceptable. One candidate recorded about thirty edit operations with pixel counts and visual notes before presentation.
- **Evidence:** The person rejected the eye shape of that candidate and, earlier, faces the agent had judged intact. The next accepted direction came from comparing the host's original face at magnification and changing only a small region, not from continuing edits on the rejected result.
- **Established result:** Self-review caught measurable defects but did not predict the person's judgment of expression. Recorded edit effort did not indicate progress toward acceptance.
- **Transfer limit:** The specific visual choices do not transfer. The transferable point is to re-anchor on the source after a rejection and to report self-review only as inspection.
